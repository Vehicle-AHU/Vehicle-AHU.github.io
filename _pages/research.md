My research focuses on robust visual perception with heterogeneous sensors. I am particularly interested in methods that can remain effective when sensing modalities exhibit spatial misalignment, semantic discrepancy, degradation, sparsity, or missing information.

## Research Directions

### Multimodal Foundation Model Pre-training

I study representation learning and cross-modal pre-training for heterogeneous visual sensing, including RGB, thermal, and event modalities.

### Multimodal Object Detection

My work investigates multimodal object detection in complex scenes, with emphasis on cross-modal alignment, fusion, UAV perception, and small/oriented objects.
