My research focuses on robust visual perception with heterogeneous sensors. I am particularly interested in methods that can remain effective when sensing modalities exhibit spatial misalignment, semantic discrepancy, degradation, sparsity, or missing information.

## Research Directions

### Multimodal RGB–Thermal Perception

I study representation learning, cross-modal alignment, fusion, and reasoning for RGB–thermal vision, especially for object detection in complex UAV scenes.

### UAV Object Detection

My work investigates small and oriented object detection from aerial platforms, with emphasis on multimodal sensing and challenging acquisition conditions.

### Event-based Vision

I am also interested in event-camera perception and RGB–event learning, including representation learning, detection, recognition, and multimodal pre-training.
